---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Get a Posy page live on posy.page."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [posy.page](https://posy.page/signup) and create an account (email and password, or GitHub or Google).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your address">
    Pick a username. Your page will live at `posy.page/<username>`. Usernames are 3 to 30 letters, numbers, `-` or `_`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your links">
    In the studio, paste a link, an email address, an `owner/repo` or a short note. Posy turns it into the right tile: a GitHub repo card, a YouTube ticket, a Spotify record, a social sticker, a plain link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a look">
    Open the design panel and pick a [look](/looks), a palette and a layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Your edits are saved as a **draft**. Press **Publish** to make them live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer to let an AI do it? [Connect an AI app](/agents/connect), or open [posy.page/new](https://posy.page/new) and copy the prompt for your chat. No account is needed to preview.
</Tip>

Running your own server instead? See [Self-hosting](/self-host/guide). The first account you create owns the instance.
