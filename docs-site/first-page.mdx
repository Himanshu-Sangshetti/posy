<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the studio">
    Paste links one after another. Drag tiles to reorder them. Use a `heading` tile to start a new section. Tiles come in four sizes: `s` (1×1), `w` (2×1), `t` (1×2) and `l` (2×2).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With an AI">
    Connect Posy to your AI app ([guide](/agents/connect)) and say: "Make me a link in bio page. My GitHub is `owner/name` and my newsletter is …". The AI works on your draft and asks before it publishes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a file or a link">
    Any chat can write a [page file](/agents/format). Open [`/new`](https://posy.page/new) and paste it, or hand over one link:

    ```
    https://posy.page/new?name=Mira%20Sol&bio=Illustrator&links=https://github.com/mira,hi@mira.so,mira/atlas
    ```

    It opens a live preview, and one button makes the page yours.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Tile types

| Type | What you give it | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| `link` | A URL | A link tile with title, description and preview image. |
| `social` | A platform and handle | A sticker or button for the profile. |
| `text` | A note | A note. `**bold**`, `*italic*` and `[links](https://example.com)` work. |
| `heading` | A title | A full-width section title. |
| `image` | An image URL or upload | A picture. |
| `video` | A YouTube URL | A video tile. |
| `music` | A Spotify URL | A track, album, playlist, artist or show. |
| `github` | `owner/name` | Stars, description and language are filled in. |

Colours are `auto`, `surface`, `accent` or a pastel tint: `peach`, `butter`, `mint`, `sky`, `lilac`, `rose`.

## Layouts

`bento` is the grid, `list` is one column of links, and `canvas` places every tile freely. Cobalt and Night follow your layout choice. The Desk, Washi, Lofi and Painted scenes lay tiles out themselves.
