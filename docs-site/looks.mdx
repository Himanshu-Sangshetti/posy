A **look** decides how your tiles are drawn: a polaroid on a desk, a window on a desktop, a print in two inks. Your content stays the same when you switch. Each look comes with four palettes, and the first is the default.

<Note>
  On posy.page, **Painted** (the default), **Desk** and **Cobalt** are free and the other three are Pro. On a self-hosted Posy every look is unlocked.
</Note>
