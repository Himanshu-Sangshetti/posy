---
title: "Looks gallery"
description: "The six looks of a Posy page. Each draws the same tiles as its own objects."
---

A **look** decides how your tiles are drawn. Switching look never loses content. Each look has four palettes; the first is the default.

On posy.page, **Desk** and **Cobalt** are free and the other four are Pro. On a self-hosted instance everything is unlocked.

## Desk

<Frame caption="Desk">
  <img src="/images/looks/cozy.jpg" alt="The Desk look" />
</Frame>

Polaroids and notes in the afternoon sun. Use `look: "cozy"` in a [page file](/agents/format) or `style: "cozy"` in the API.

Palettes: cream, oak, sage, slate.

## Washi

<Frame caption="Washi">
  <img src="/images/looks/washi.jpg" alt="The Washi look" />
</Frame>

Paper, ink and a red seal. Use `look: "washi"` in a [page file](/agents/format) or `style: "washi"` in the API.

Palettes: kinari, sakura, yomogi, sumi.

## Lofi

<Frame caption="Lofi">
  <img src="/images/looks/lofi.jpg" alt="The Lofi look" />

A desktop left open, with windows you can drag. Use `look: "lofi"` in a [page file](/agents/format) or `style: "lofi"` in the API.

Palettes: daydream, night-blue, violet, sunset.

## Painted

<Frame caption="Painted">

## Cobalt

A vintage print in two inks, under a crescent moon. Use `look: "cobalt"` in a [page file](/agents/format) or `style: "cobalt"` in the API.

## Night

<Frame caption="Night">

</Frame>

Palettes: netrunner, ice, redline, daylight.

<Note>
  Older pages made with the retired `press` and `gallery` looks open as Cobalt and Painted.
</Note>
